Pyramid Craft

An offline pyramid building game for iPhone

Pyramid Craft lets you build the pyramids of ancient Egypt yourself: dig Nile mud, mould and dry bricks, carry them up and lay three monuments layer by layer. Fully offline.

Most pyramid games let you place a finished pyramid with one tap. Pyramid Craft goes the other way: you walk the building site in first person and every brick passes through your hands, from the mud of the Nile to its square in the layer.

What you do

Make the bricks

Dig mud on the river bank, pour it into a wooden mould and let the sun dry it. Dry bricks turn pale; take them off the table.

Lay the monument

Carry the bricks to the monument and lay them on the marked squares. Each layer sits one square inside the one below, so the monument steps up as you go.

Grow the workshop

Build a store, bigger mould tables, ramps and a hand cart. Later, water wheels on the Nile turn conveyor belts that carry mud and bricks for you.

Lead a crew

Workers join you and take jobs: digging mud, moulding, carrying and laying. Each needs a hut of their own.

Three monuments

Mudbrick Tomb

A mastaba-like tomb of 1,240 mud bricks in five layers: the place to learn every step.

Sky Stairway

A stepped pyramid of 5,456 bricks in sixteen layers, in pale grey limestone.

The Great Pyramid

26,235 bricks in twenty-seven layers, finished with a smooth white casing and a gilded capstone.

Why it stays calm

  • Fully offline: no account, no internet needed.
  • No timers or energy bars: play five minutes or an hour.
  • A slow sunset over the dunes and the Nile, with soft desert music.
  • Your site is saved in your own iCloud and follows you to a new iPhone.
