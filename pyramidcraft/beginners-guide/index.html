Pyramid Craft beginner's guide: 10 tips

Ten tips for your first hour in Pyramid Craft: from the first mud brick to ramps, stores, workers and water wheels.

1. Look around first

Drag on the empty right side of the screen to look up, down and around; the stick on the left walks.

2. Follow the gold arrow

At the start a gold arrow and a task line show the next step: the mud, the mould table, the dry bricks, the next white square.

3. Keep the table busy

While bricks dry, fetch the next load of mud. A full table and full hands are the fastest rhythm.

4. Lay from the middle out

Fill the inner squares of a layer before the edges, so you can always reach the open ones.

5. Jump up one step

With bricks in hand you climb only low steps; the Jump button takes you up one brick.

6. Fill the store

Bricks kept in the store pay for every building, from ramps to bigger tables, and come back in full if you take a building down.

7. Upgrade the mould table

A bigger table dries more bricks at once: one of the best uses of your first stored bricks.

8. Spend mastery on Strong Arms

Laying bricks earns mastery points. Strong Arms lets you carry more on every trip.

9. Ramps for the high layers

Ramps let you walk up loaded and push the hand cart to the top of the monument.

10. Give your workers jobs

Look at a worker and give a job. A digger, a moulder and a mason together keep bricks coming while you build.
