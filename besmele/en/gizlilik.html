Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026
In short: Besmele does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. No account is needed. The app contains no ads, tracking or analytics.

Information that stays on your phone

Your reading progress, learned names, saved passages, selected city and settings are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to us or to any other server. Deleting the app deletes all of them.

Location

Location access is optional. If you allow it, your location is used only on your phone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. If you don’t, you can pick your city from the list and the app still works.

When you tap “Use my location”, Apple’s map service is used to find your city’s name; when you search for a city, it is used to show results. These are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. Besmele does not receive or store this information.

Notifications

Prayer time, daily reminder and holy day notifications are prepared on your phone. No server is used to send them. You can turn them off at any time in the app’s Settings or in your phone’s settings.

Sharing

When you share a passage, the image card is created on your phone. You decide whom and with which app to share it.

Children

Besmele does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and the date above will be updated.
