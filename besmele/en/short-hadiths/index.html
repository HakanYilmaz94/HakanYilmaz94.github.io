99 Short Hadiths with English Meaning and Source

Below are 99 short hadiths with their English meaning and source; some include the Arabic text. They are the hadiths the Bismillah app shows one day at a time.

  1. إِنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ

    “Actions are only by intentions.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  2. يَسِّرُوا وَلَا تُعَسِّرُوا وَبَشِّرُوا وَلَا تُنَفِّرُوا

    “Make things easy, not difficult; give good news, do not drive people away.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  3. الْمُسْلِمُ مَنْ سَلِمَ الْمُسْلِمُونَ مِنْ لِسَانِهِ وَيَدِهِ

    “A Muslim is one from whose tongue and hand other Muslims are safe.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  4. لَا يُؤْمِنُ أَحَدُكُمْ حَتَّى يُحِبَّ لِأَخِيهِ مَا يُحِبُّ لِنَفْسِهِ

    “None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  5. مَنْ كَانَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الْآخِرِ فَلْيَقُلْ خَيْرًا أَوْ لِيَصْمُتْ

    “Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, let him speak good or remain silent.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  6. الْكَلِمَةُ الطَّيِّبَةُ صَدَقَةٌ

    “A good word is charity.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  7. تَبَسُّمُكَ فِي وَجْهِ أَخِيكَ لَكَ صَدَقَةٌ

    “Your smile to your brother is charity.”

    Tirmidhi

  8. مَنْ لَا يَرْحَمُ لَا يُرْحَمُ

    “Whoever does not show mercy will not be shown mercy.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  9. “The strong one is not the one who wins at wrestling, but the one who controls himself in anger.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  10. “Allah does not look at your appearance or your wealth, but He looks at your hearts and your deeds.”

    Muslim

  11. أَحَبُّ الْأَعْمَالِ إِلَى اللَّهِ أَدْوَمُهَا وَإِنْ قَلَّ

    “The most beloved deeds to Allah are those done consistently, even if small.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  12. الدِّينُ النَّصِيحَةُ

    “Religion is sincerity.”

    Muslim

  13. الْحَيَاءُ مِنَ الْإِيمَانِ

    “Modesty is part of faith.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  14. الطُّهُورُ شَطْرُ الْإِيمَانِ

    “Cleanliness is half of faith.”

    Muslim

  15. مَنْ لَا يَشْكُرُ النَّاسَ لَا يَشْكُرُ اللَّهَ

    “Whoever does not thank people does not thank Allah.”

    Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi

  16. مَا نَقَصَتْ صَدَقَةٌ مِنْ مَالٍ

    “Charity does not decrease wealth.”

    Muslim

  17. خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ

    “The best of you are those who learn the Qur’an and teach it.”

    Bukhari

  18. لَا يُلْدَغُ الْمُؤْمِنُ مِنْ جُحْرٍ وَاحِدٍ مَرَّتَيْنِ

    “A believer is not stung from the same hole twice.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  19. مَنْ دَلَّ عَلَى خَيْرٍ فَلَهُ مِثْلُ أَجْرِ فَاعِلِهِ

    “Whoever guides to good earns a reward like the one who does it.”

    Muslim

  20. “There are two blessings many people waste: health and free time.”

    Bukhari

  21. الْمَرْءُ مَعَ مَنْ أَحَبَّ

    “A person will be with those they love.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  22. كُنْ فِي الدُّنْيَا كَأَنَّكَ غَرِيبٌ أَوْ عَابِرُ سَبِيلٍ

    “Be in this world as if you were a stranger or a traveler.”

    Bukhari

  23. لَا تَغْضَبْ

    “Do not get angry.”

    Bukhari

  24. إِنَّ مِنْ خِيَارِكُمْ أَحْسَنَكُمْ أَخْلَاقًا

    “The best of you are those with the best character.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  25. وَكُونُوا عِبَادَ اللَّهِ إِخْوَانًا

    “O servants of Allah, be brothers.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  26. إِنَّ اللَّهَ جَمِيلٌ يُحِبُّ الْجَمَالَ

    “Allah is beautiful and loves beauty.”

    Muslim

  27. وَمَا تَوَاضَعَ أَحَدٌ لِلَّهِ إِلَّا رَفَعَهُ اللَّهُ

    “No one humbles himself for Allah except that Allah raises him.”

    Muslim

  28. “The strong believer is better and more beloved to Allah than the weak believer, yet there is good in both.”

    Muslim

  29. “When a person dies, his deeds end except for three: ongoing charity, beneficial knowledge, and a righteous child who prays for him.”

    Muslim

  30. خَيْرُكُمْ خَيْرُكُمْ لِأَهْلِهِ

    “The best of you are those who are best to their families.”

    Tirmidhi

  31. “He is not of us who sleeps full while his neighbor goes hungry.”

    Bukhari, al-Adab al-Mufrad

  32. أَفْشُوا السَّلَامَ بَيْنَكُمْ

    “Spread greetings of peace among yourselves.”

    Muslim

  33. تَهَادَوْا تَحَابُّوا

    “Give gifts to one another, and you will love one another.”

    Bukhari, al-Adab al-Mufrad

  34. “Whoever relieves a believer of a hardship of this world, Allah will relieve him of a hardship on the Day of Resurrection.”

    Muslim

  35. وَاللَّهُ فِي عَوْنِ الْعَبْدِ مَا كَانَ الْعَبْدُ فِي عَوْنِ أَخِيهِ

    “Allah helps His servant as long as the servant helps his brother.”

    Muslim

  36. “The believers in their love and mercy toward one another are like one body: when one part aches, the whole body shares its pain.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  37. الْمُؤْمِنُ لِلْمُؤْمِنِ كَالْبُنْيَانِ يَشُدُّ بَعْضُهُ بَعْضًا

    “A believer to another believer is like the bricks of a building, each supporting the other.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  38. إِذَا لَمْ تَسْتَحْيِ فَاصْنَعْ مَا شِئْتَ

    “If you feel no shame, then do as you wish.”

    Bukhari

  39. “The lawful is clear and the unlawful is clear, and between them are doubtful matters. Whoever avoids the doubtful protects his religion and honor.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  40. مِنْ حُسْنِ إِسْلَامِ الْمَرْءِ تَرْكُهُ مَا لَا يَعْنِيهِ

    “Part of a person’s good Islam is leaving what does not concern him.”

    Tirmidhi

  41. دَعْ مَا يَرِيبُكَ إِلَى مَا لَا يَرِيبُكَ

    “Leave what makes you doubt for what does not make you doubt.”

    Tirmidhi, Nasa’i

  42. “Be mindful of Allah wherever you are. Follow a bad deed with a good one to erase it, and treat people with good character.”

    Tirmidhi

  43. احْفَظِ اللَّهَ يَحْفَظْكَ

    “Be mindful of Allah, and He will protect you.”

    Tirmidhi

  44. إِذَا سَأَلْتَ فَاسْأَلِ اللَّهَ وَإِذَا اسْتَعَنْتَ فَاسْتَعِنْ بِاللَّهِ

    “When you ask, ask Allah; when you seek help, seek help from Allah.”

    Tirmidhi

  45. لَا ضَرَرَ وَلَا ضِرَارَ

    “There should be neither harming nor reciprocating harm.”

    Ibn Majah

  46. أَكْثِرُوا ذِكْرَ هَاذِمِ اللَّذَّاتِ

    “Remember often the destroyer of pleasures: death.”

    Tirmidhi, Nasa’i

  47. “Richness is not having many possessions; true richness is the richness of the soul.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  48. الْيَدُ الْعُلْيَا خَيْرٌ مِنَ الْيَدِ السُّفْلَى

    “The upper hand (that gives) is better than the lower hand (that takes).”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  49. اتَّقُوا النَّارَ وَلَوْ بِشِقِّ تَمْرَةٍ

    “Protect yourselves from the Fire, even with half a date in charity.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  50. أَعْطُوا الْأَجِيرَ أَجْرَهُ قَبْلَ أَنْ يَجِفَّ عَرَقُهُ

    “Give the worker his wage before his sweat dries.”

    Ibn Majah

  51. لَيْسَ مِنَّا مَنْ لَمْ يَرْحَمْ صَغِيرَنَا وَيُوَقِّرْ كَبِيرَنَا

    “He is not of us who does not show mercy to our young and respect to our elders.”

    Tirmidhi

  52. الدُّعَاءُ هُوَ الْعِبَادَةُ

    “Supplication is worship itself.”

    Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi

  53. “Whoever builds a mosque for the sake of Allah, Allah will build for him a house in Paradise.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  54. أَقْرَبُ مَا يَكُونُ الْعَبْدُ مِنْ رَبِّهِ وَهُوَ سَاجِدٌ

    “A servant is closest to his Lord while in prostration.”

    Muslim

  55. “The five daily prayers are like a river at your door in which you bathe five times a day; no dirt remains.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  56. “Prayer in congregation is twenty-seven degrees better than praying alone.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  57. الصِّيَامُ جُنَّةٌ

    “Fasting is a shield.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  58. تَسَحَّرُوا فَإِنَّ فِي السَّحُورِ بَرَكَةً

    “Eat the pre-dawn meal, for in it there is blessing.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  59. “The fasting person has two joys: when he breaks his fast and when he meets his Lord.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  60. “Islam is built on five: bearing witness that there is no god but Allah and that Muhammad is His Messenger, prayer, charity, pilgrimage, and fasting in Ramadan.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  61. “In the body there is a piece of flesh; if it is sound, the whole body is sound, and if it is corrupt, the whole body is corrupt. Truly, it is the heart.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  62. “Faith has seventy-some branches. The highest is saying ‘There is no god but Allah’, and the lowest is removing something harmful from the road.”

    Muslim

  63. كُلُّ مَعْرُوفٍ صَدَقَةٌ

    “Every good deed is charity.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  64. “Allah said: My mercy prevails over My wrath.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  65. “Allah said: If My servant draws near to Me a hand’s span, I draw near to him an arm’s length. If he comes walking, I come running.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  66. الْمَرْءُ عَلَى دِينِ خَلِيلِهِ

    “A person follows the way of his close friend, so be careful whom you befriend.”

    Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi

  67. “The most beloved words to Allah are four: SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, La ilaha illallah and Allahu Akbar.”

    Muslim

  68. “Two phrases are light on the tongue, heavy on the scale and beloved to the Most Merciful: SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi, SubhanAllahil-‘Azim.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  69. “It is not lawful for a Muslim to stay estranged from his brother for more than three days.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  70. “Do not envy one another and do not turn your backs on one another. O servants of Allah, be brothers.”

    Muslim

  71. “Beware of suspicion, for suspicion is the most false of speech.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  72. إِنَّ الصِّدْقَ يَهْدِي إِلَى الْبِرِّ

    “Truthfulness leads to righteousness.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  73. “The signs of a hypocrite are three: when he speaks he lies, when he promises he breaks it, and when trusted he betrays.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  74. “Whoever wishes for his provision to be increased and his life to be blessed, let him keep good ties with his relatives.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  75. “A man asked: ‘Who most deserves my good companionship?’ The Prophet said: ‘Your mother, then your mother, then your mother, then your father.’”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  76. “The pleasure of the Lord lies in the pleasure of the parent.”

    Tirmidhi

  77. “A Muslim has five rights over another: returning his greeting, visiting him when sick, attending his funeral, accepting his invitation, and blessing him when he sneezes.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  78. “Allah forgave a man who gave water to a dog dying of thirst. When asked if there is reward in kindness to animals, the Prophet said: ‘There is reward in kindness to every living being.’”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  79. ارْحَمُوا مَنْ فِي الْأَرْضِ يَرْحَمْكُمْ مَنْ فِي السَّمَاءِ

    “Show mercy to those on earth, and the One in heaven will show mercy to you.”

    Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi

  80. إِنَّ الرِّفْقَ لَا يَكُونُ فِي شَيْءٍ إِلَّا زَانَهُ

    “Gentleness is not found in anything except that it beautifies it.”

    Muslim

  81. إِنَّ اللَّهَ رَفِيقٌ يُحِبُّ الرِّفْقَ

    “Allah is gentle and loves gentleness in all matters.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  82. “How wonderful is the affair of the believer! All of it is good. If something good happens he is grateful, and that is good for him; if hardship befalls him he is patient, and that is good for him.”

    Muslim

  83. “No fatigue, illness, sorrow or hardship befalls a believer, not even a thorn that pricks him, except that it expiates some of his sins.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  84. إِنَّمَا الصَّبْرُ عِنْدَ الصَّدْمَةِ الْأُولَى

    “True patience is at the first moment of a calamity.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  85. “Whoever takes a path seeking knowledge, Allah makes easy for him a path to Paradise.”

    Muslim

  86. مَنْ يُرِدِ اللَّهُ بِهِ خَيْرًا يُفَقِّهْهُ فِي الدِّينِ

    “When Allah wills good for someone, He gives him understanding of the religion.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  87. “Read the Qur’an, for it will come on the Day of Resurrection as an intercessor for its companions.”

    Muslim

  88. خَيْرُ يَوْمٍ طَلَعَتْ عَلَيْهِ الشَّمْسُ يَوْمُ الْجُمُعَةِ

    “The best day on which the sun has risen is Friday.”

    Muslim

  89. مَنْ صَلَّى عَلَيَّ وَاحِدَةً صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ عَشْرًا

    “Whoever sends blessings upon me once, Allah sends blessings upon him tenfold.”

    Muslim

  90. “Allah extends His hand by night so that the one who sinned by day may repent, and extends His hand by day so that the one who sinned by night may repent.”

    Muslim

  91. كُلُّ ابْنِ آدَمَ خَطَّاءٌ وَخَيْرُ الْخَطَّائِينَ التَّوَّابُونَ

    “Every child of Adam errs, and the best of those who err are those who repent.”

    Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah

  92. “No one fills a vessel worse than his stomach; a few morsels to keep him upright are enough.”

    Tirmidhi

  93. “Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, let him honor his guest.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  94. “Gabriel kept urging me to be kind to the neighbor until I thought he would make him an heir.”

    Bukhari, Muslim

  95. “On the Day of Resurrection a servant’s feet will not move until he is asked about his life and how he spent it, his knowledge and what he did with it, his wealth and how he earned and spent it, and his body and how he used it.”

    Tirmidhi

  96. “Whoever of you sees a wrong, let him change it with his hand; if he cannot, then with his tongue; and if he cannot, then with his heart.”

    Muslim

  97. “Hearts rust as iron rusts, and their polish is the remembrance of Allah.”

    Bayhaqi

  98. الصَّلَاةُ نُورٌ

    “Prayer is light.”

    Muslim

  99. “No Muslim plants a tree or sows a crop from which a person, bird or animal eats, except that it is charity for him.”

    Bukhari, Muslim
