About the studio

HY Apps is an independent studio: one developer who designs, codes and draws every app and game. Our games are calm, fair and private: no accounts, no tracking for advertising, everything explained in plain words. Pages and apps are in English and Turkish; newer games add German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.
