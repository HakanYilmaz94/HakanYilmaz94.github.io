Glowfly

A Relaxing Idle Merge Game for iPhone That Plays While You're Away

Looking for a calm idle game with no forced ads? In Glowfly, lights bounce through the dark, merge into brighter ones and reveal a hand-drawn night sky, even while the game is closed.

An idle game is one that keeps making progress on its own: you make a few good choices, and the game does the rest, even while your phone is in your pocket. A merge game adds one simple rule: combine identical things into one better thing. Glowfly puts both ideas under a quiet night sky.

How Glowfly plays

Lights that bounce

Each light flies in a straight line, bounces off the walls and breaks the glass and rock it hits. Every broken piece reveals a little more of the night landscape underneath.

Ten become one

Collect ten lights of the same kind and merge them into one brighter, stronger light. There are twelve kinds of light, from a soft spark to the brightest star.

A night at a time

When every wall is broken, the whole landscape lights up and the next night begins. Every ten nights a fallen star is found and a postcard joins your story.

A story to the end

Ten chapters, from a forest cabin to a lighthouse, a desert, a sleeping town, the deep sea, the planets and the Milky Way. Night 1,000 completes the sky.

Why it stays relaxing

  • It plays while you're away: your lights keep working for hours after you close the game, and you come back to nights already cleared.
  • No ad ever plays on its own. Some rewards can be earned by choosing to watch a short video, and that's all.
  • No timers chasing you, no energy bars and no lives. Open it for a minute or an hour.
  • Soft lo-fi music and gentle glass sounds, with options for fewer effects and no flashing.
  • Your progress is backed up in your own iCloud and follows you to a new iPhone or iPad.

Who it is for

Players who like to check in

If you enjoy opening a game for a few minutes, collecting what built up and making one or two choices, Glowfly is made for that rhythm.

Players who like to watch

Hold the speed button and watch a wall of glass shatter under dozens of lights, or tap the rocks yourself. It is satisfying without asking for skill.

Anyone who wants a calm screen

Dark, quiet colors, a slow story and no pressure: it is a pleasant thing to leave running.

Glowfly is free to play from the first night to the last, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
