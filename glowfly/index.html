Is Glowfly free?

Yes, Glowfly is free to play from the first night to the last. Glowfly Plus (an optional subscription), an ad-free option and gem packs are available as in-app purchases; prices are always shown on the button before you buy.

Does the game keep going when it's closed?

Yes. Your lights keep breaking walls for 4 hours after you leave (longer with Deep Sleep or Glowfly Plus). When you come back, the game shows how many nights were cleared and the light you earned.

Are there ads?

Only if you want them. Some rewards, like a boost, can be earned by watching a short video ad. No ad ever plays on its own, and with Plus or the ad-free option you get those rewards without ads.

What is in a Star Box?

Boxes drop from the sky now and then and at every chapter end. The game lists exactly what a box can contain and the chance of each prize before you open one.

Will my progress move to my new phone?

Yes. The game keeps a backup in your own iCloud. Purchases come back with Restore Purchases on the Glowfly Plus screen.

Is it suitable for children?

Glowfly has no violence, no chat and no account. In-app purchases are protected by your Apple Account and Screen Time settings.
