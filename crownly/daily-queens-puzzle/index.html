How to play Queens

  1. Place exactly one crown in every row and every column.
  2. Place exactly one crown in every colored region.
  3. Crowns may not touch each other, not even diagonally.
  4. Use dots to mark cells that can't hold a crown. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, so you never have to guess.
