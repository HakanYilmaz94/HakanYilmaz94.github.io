LinkedIn Games Alternatives: Queens, Zip, Patches, Tango and Mini Sudoku Outside LinkedIn

Love LinkedIn's daily games but want more than one puzzle a day, or want to play without opening LinkedIn? Here is what each game is and where to find the same kind of puzzle.

LinkedIn's games are short daily logic and word puzzles. Each one is a classic puzzle type with a new name, which means you can find the same kind of puzzle elsewhere, often with unlimited boards.

Queens → Star Battle

One crown in every row, column and colored region, and crowns never touch. The classic name is Star Battle. In Crownly it is Crowns: a daily board, 6,500 levels and hints that explain the logic.

Zip → one-line path puzzle

Connect numbered stops in order with one line through every cell. In Crownly it is Route, with walls, a daily board and levels in four difficulties.

Patches → Shikaku

Split the grid into rectangles, each holding one number equal to its area. In Crownly it is Mosaic, with a 7×7 and a 10×10 daily board.

Tango → binary puzzle (Takuzu)

Fill a grid with two symbols so rows and columns balance and no three in a row match. The classic is called Takuzu or Binairo; many free puzzle apps have it. Crownly does not have this one.

Mini Sudoku → 6×6 sudoku

A smaller sudoku with 2×3 boxes. Any sudoku app with a 6×6 size gives you the same puzzle. Crownly does not have sudoku; if you like it, Queens is a good next step (see our sudoku-like games guide).

Three of them in one app

Crownly puts Crowns, Route and Mosaic together on iPhone and iPad, each with a new daily board at 07:00 UTC, streaks, leaderboards and live duels with friends. Free, with an optional one-time Pro purchase, no ads.

Questions

Can I play LinkedIn Queens without LinkedIn?

LinkedIn's own boards are only on LinkedIn, but the same kind of puzzle (Star Battle) is in other apps. Crownly has a daily Queens-rules board, 6,500 levels and a free daily puzzle on hyapps.app.

Is there one app with Queens, Zip and Patches style puzzles?

Crownly has all three kinds: Crowns (Queens rules), Route (Zip-style path) and Mosaic (Shikaku, like Patches), each with a daily board.
