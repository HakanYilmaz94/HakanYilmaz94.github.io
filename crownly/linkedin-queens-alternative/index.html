Crownly

LinkedIn Queens Alternative: Play Queens Every Day on iPhone

Love the LinkedIn Queens game? Crownly is a free iPhone app with a new Queens puzzle every day, 2,000 levels and hints that explain the logic.

LinkedIn's Queens is one puzzle a day, and then it's over until tomorrow. If you want more, Crownly gives you the same kind of puzzle with the same rules (one crown in every row, column and region, no touching), designed as a calm iPhone game.

What you get in Crownly

  • A new daily puzzle at the same time for everyone, plus a 10×10 Daily Hard.
  • 2,000 levels from Easy to Expert, with up to three stars each.
  • Hints that explain the next logical step instead of revealing the answer.
  • A Logic School with six interactive lessons.
  • Streaks, streak freezes, Game Center leaderboards and widgets.
  • Color-blind mode, dark mode, no ads, works offline.

Crownly is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn. Queens and Star Battle are names of a puzzle type.
