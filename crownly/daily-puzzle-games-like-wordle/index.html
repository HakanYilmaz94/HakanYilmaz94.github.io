Daily Puzzle Games Like Wordle: 7 to Play Every Morning

Loved Wordle? Seven daily puzzles with one new board a day, from word games to logic grids like Queens, and where to play them on iPhone.

Wordle's magic was simple: one puzzle a day, the same for everyone, done in a few minutes, then a small result to share. Plenty of games now follow that rhythm. Here are seven, word and logic.

Word puzzles

  • Wordle (New York Times Games): guess a five-letter word in six tries.
  • Connections (New York Times Games): sort sixteen words into four hidden groups.

Logic puzzles

  • Queens (LinkedIn): one crown in every row, column and colored region; crowns never touch.
  • Zip (LinkedIn): draw one path through the numbered stops in order, filling every cell.
  • Tango (LinkedIn): fill the grid with two symbols so rows and columns balance.
  • A daily sudoku: most sudoku apps and newspapers publish one board a day.

Crownly: three daily logic puzzles in one app

Crownly puts three of these ideas together on iPhone and iPad: Crowns (Queens-style), Route (a one-line path like Zip) and Mosaic (split the grid into numbered rectangles). Each has a new daily board at 07:00 UTC, the same for everyone, with a streak, leaderboards and an archive of past days.

Beyond the daily boards there are 6,500 levels, hints that explain the logic instead of giving the answer, and live duels with 2 to 4 friends. It is free with an optional one-time Pro purchase, has no ads and works offline.
