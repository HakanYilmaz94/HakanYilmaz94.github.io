LinkedIn Zip Alternative: More One-Line Path Puzzles on iPhone

Finished today's Zip and want more? Route in Crownly is the same kind of puzzle: connect the numbered stops in order with one line through every cell, every day and in thousands of levels.

Zip is over in a minute, and the next one comes tomorrow. If you like drawing that single line through every cell, Crownly's Route gives you the same kind of puzzle whenever you want it.

Same idea, more boards

  • One line from stop 1 to the last stop, visiting the numbers in order.
  • The line fills every cell exactly once and never crosses a wall.
  • A new daily Route board at 07:00 UTC, the same for everyone, with a streak and leaderboards.
  • Route levels in four difficulties, from 5×5 warm-ups to big 9×9 boards.
  • Every board has exactly one solution, so you never need to guess.

When you get stuck

Hints don't just draw the answer: the first tap shows the next safe move and why it is forced, the second applies it. That is how the corners-first and no-pockets techniques become habits.

Two more puzzles in the same app

Crownly also has Crowns (Queens rules: one crown per row, column and colored region) and Mosaic (Shikaku: split the grid into numbered rectangles), each with its own daily board. It is free with an optional one-time Pro purchase, has no ads and works offline.

Questions

Is there an app like LinkedIn Zip?

Crownly's Route is a one-line path puzzle with the same kind of rules: connect numbered stops in order with one line that covers every cell. It has a daily board and levels in four difficulties.

Can I play more than one Zip-style puzzle a day?

Yes. Besides the daily Route board, Crownly has Route levels you can play any time, plus an archive of past daily puzzles with Pro.
