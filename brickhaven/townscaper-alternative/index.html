Brickhaven

Townscaper Alternative: A Cozy Town Builder With a Story (iPhone and iPad)

Love building pretty seaside towns in Townscaper but want goals and a story? Brickhaven lets you rebuild a seaside town brick by brick, then design and decorate your own streets.

Townscaper is a beautiful toy: tap and a little seaside town grows by itself. Some players love that it has no goals; others finish an afternoon wishing there was something to work toward. Brickhaven is for the second group.

Townscaper and Brickhaven side by side

  • Townscaper: tap to place blocks, the game shapes them into houses; no goals, no story.
  • Brickhaven: walk your seaside town in first person and lay every brick yourself.
  • Brickhaven has nine story buildings, each with an owner who comes home when it is finished.
  • After the story, design your own houses at the Architect's Table and decorate the streets freely.
  • Both are calm, with no timers; Brickhaven is offline, ad-free, and the first two buildings are free.

Who will like Brickhaven

If the slow, hands-on part is what you enjoy, carrying bricks, watching a wall rise row by row, seeing the whole building replayed at the end, Brickhaven gives you hours of it with a gentle goal always in front of you.

Questions

Is there a game like Townscaper with goals?

Brickhaven is a cozy seaside town builder with a story and goals: rebuild nine buildings brick by brick, then design and decorate your own.

No, it plays fully offline on iPhone and iPad.
