Games to Play on a Plane Without Internet: An iPhone and iPad Checklist

The best games for a flight are ones that need no internet, no account check and no timers. Download and open them at home, restore purchases before you leave, and switch on Low Power Mode on board. Brickhaven, a cozy brick building game, plays fully offline on iPhone and iPad.

Airplane Wi-Fi is slow, expensive or missing, and many games quietly need a connection: to log in, to load levels, to show ads or to check a purchase. A few minutes of preparation at home makes the difference between a calm flight and a screen that says "No connection".

Before you leave home

  • Install the games while you are on Wi-Fi; large games download extra content on first launch.
  • Open every game once and play a minute, so first-run downloads, tutorials and sign-ins are done.
  • Restore or make any purchases at home; the App Store cannot confirm a new purchase in the air.
  • Update the games a day before, not at the gate.
  • Charge the phone and pack a cable or power bank; 3D games use more battery than puzzles.

On board

  • Switch on Airplane Mode early: the phone stops searching for signal and saves battery.
  • Turn on Low Power Mode and lower the brightness a little.
  • Use headphones or play muted; most cozy games are fine without sound.
  • Pick a game you can pause at any moment: meal service and landing never wait.

What makes a good flight game

It works with no connection at all, it saves often, it has no timers or energy that run out, and you can stop in the middle without losing anything. Building, puzzle and story games with a steady pace fit best; online multiplayer games and games with forced ads usually do not work offline.

Brickhaven on a flight

Brickhaven is a cozy first-person building game: you find the right brick in the pile, carry it and lay it, rebuilding a seaside town one building at a time. It plays fully offline, has no ads and no timers, and saves every few seconds, so you can close it the moment the seatbelt sign comes on.

The first two buildings are free, and a building takes a good while, enough for a short flight. On a long one, the Architect's Table lets you design your own house and build it on your lot.

Another offline game from the same studio

Birbdoku, a cozy logic puzzle with two cockatiels (one bird in every row, column and color), also plays offline; only the weekly season ranking and friends update when you are back online.

Questions

Which iPhone games work in Airplane Mode?

Any game that does not need an account check, online levels or ads to start. Brickhaven and Birbdoku both play offline; open them once at home before the flight.

Does Brickhaven need Wi-Fi?

No. Brickhaven plays fully offline. Purchases, iCloud sync and Game Center simply wait until you are online again.

How do I save battery while gaming on a plane?

Turn on Airplane Mode and Low Power Mode, lower the brightness and play with headphones or muted. Brickhaven also makes its graphics lighter by itself in Low Power Mode.
