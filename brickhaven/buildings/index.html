All 9 Buildings in Brickhaven: Owners, Order and What Makes Each One Special

The nine story buildings of Brickhaven in order, from Martha's Bakery to the Train Station: who lives there, what is new in each and which ones are free.

Brickhaven's story has nine buildings. You build them in order, every brick by hand, and each one is bigger or taller than the last: the Bakery takes about 500 bricks, the Fire Station about 1,400. The first two are free; the Whole Town, a one-time purchase, opens the other seven.

1. Bakery

Martha the baker. The first building and the tutorial: pink walls, a tiled roof and a chimney. You learn to read the blueprint, carry bricks and ride the lift. Free.

2. Red Barn

Farmer Joe. Almost twice the bricks, with double doors and barn windows. When it is done the cows, chickens and hay bales come back. Free.

3. Flower Shop

Daisy. A two-tone shop with a fenced front garden, where Grandma Rose's roses used to come from.

4. Seaside Café

Lucy. A mint-green shop floor, a glass conservatory and a terrace you pave yourself, stone by stone.

5. Ice Cream Parlor

Mia. A flat-roofed parlor in pink and mint with a giant cone on the roof and tables outside.

6. Bookshop

Mr. Oliver. Three floors, a corner tower and a covered porch. The first building where the lift really matters.

7. Clock Tower

Arthur the clockmaker. An eleven-meter tower on the town square with a clock face on all four sides and a pointed spire. When it is finished the clock shows the real time and the bell rings.

8. Fire Station

Sam and his crew. The biggest build: two garage doors, a flat roof and a tall lookout tower, with the red truck waiting outside.

9. Train Station

Henry the station master. A long hall with a clock tower and a platform canopy. Finish it and the train calls at Brickhaven again.

After the ninth building

When the station is finished the whole town gathers on the square to celebrate. After that Brickhaven is yours to walk around, decorate, and add to with buildings you design yourself at the Architect's Table.
