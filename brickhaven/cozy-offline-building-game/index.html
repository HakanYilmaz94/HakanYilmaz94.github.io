Brickhaven

A Cozy Offline Building Game for iPhone and iPad

Looking for a relaxing game with no timers, no ads and no internet needed? Brickhaven lets you rebuild a seaside town one brick at a time.

A cozy game is one you can open after a long day without any pressure: nothing chases you, nothing runs out, and every minute you play leaves something nice behind. Brickhaven is built around that idea.

What you do in Brickhaven

Build with your own hands

Walk to the brick pile, find the right shape and color, carry it to the wall and lay it. Row by row, a bakery, a barn or a clock tower rises in front of you.

Bring the town back to life

Grandma Rose left you a seaside town that has fallen to ruin. Each finished building brings its owner home, from Martha's bakery to the train station.

Design and decorate

Draw your own house, café or shop at the Architect's Table, build it on your lot, then add benches, lamps, flower beds, trees, cats and chickens.

Why it stays calm

  • Fully offline: play on a plane, on the train or anywhere without Wi-Fi.
  • No timers, no energy bars, no lives: play five minutes or an hour, at your own pace.
  • No ads, no subscription: the first two buildings are free, the rest of the town is one purchase.
  • Your town lives in your own iCloud, so it follows you from iPhone to iPad.
  • A color-blind mode prints a symbol on every brick color.

It suits all ages: there is no violence, no chat with strangers and nothing to buy by accident.
