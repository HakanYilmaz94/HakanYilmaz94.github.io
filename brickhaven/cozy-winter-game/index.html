A Cozy Winter Game for iPhone and iPad: Snow Season in Brickhaven

From 1 December to 15 January, Brickhaven's seaside town turns white by itself: snow on the grass, roofs and roads, light strings and wreaths on every finished building, a decorated tree on the square and winter decorations to place. It is a calm, offline building game with no timers and no ads.

Winter is the season for slow evenings, warm drinks and something gentle on the screen. Brickhaven is a cozy first-person building game where you rebuild a seaside town brick by brick, and in December the whole town dresses for the holidays. Nothing to download: the winter look switches on by itself.

What changes in winter

  • Snowy grass, white hills and a pale winter sky over the town.
  • Snow on the roads and between the paving stones, and patches of snow on the beach.
  • Snow on the roof of every finished building, a string of colored lights along the eaves and a wreath on the front door.
  • A decorated tree with a star on the town square, next to the Clock Tower once you rebuild it.
  • Falling snow on the main menu (it stops if Reduce Motion is on).

Winter decorations

In Decorate, a Winter group appears during the season: a snowman, gift boxes, a lantern and a sled. Place them anywhere in town; the ones you place stay after the season ends. Decorate is part of the Whole Town.

When it starts and how to switch it off

The winter look follows your device's calendar: it is on from 1 December to 15 January, every year. If you prefer green grass, open Settings → Game → Winter look and turn it off; the switch only appears during the season, and winter comes back on by itself next year.

Why it suits a winter evening

There is no clock in Brickhaven: no timers, no energy, nothing that spoils while you are away. You lay a few rows of bricks, watch a building rise and the owner come home, and put the phone down whenever you like. It plays fully offline, so it also suits holiday trips.

Questions

When is winter in Brickhaven?

From 1 December to 15 January, by your device's calendar. It switches on and off by itself.

Do I have to pay for the winter look?

No. The snowy town is for everyone, including the free buildings. The winter decorations live in Decorate, which comes with the Whole Town.

Can I turn the snow off?

Yes: Settings → Game → Winter look. The switch appears only during the season, and winter returns by itself the next year.
