A Brick-Laying Simulator for iPhone: How Building Works in Brickhaven

Want to build walls brick by brick on your phone? How Brickhaven's bricklaying works: shapes and colors, blueprints, sections, mortar, the lift and skills.

Most building games place whole walls or rooms with one tap. Brickhaven goes the other way: every wall is made of real bricks, and you lay each one yourself. It is the closest thing to a bricklaying simulator on iPhone and iPad, but calm, with no timer and no way to fail.

Find the right brick

Bricks come in shapes (1×1, 2×1, long blocks, roof tiles, window frames, doors) and colors. The building's blueprint says which ones each section needs. Shapes must match; a wrong color can be laid but glows red until you take it back, so mistakes are always fixable.

Carry, then lay

You carry a stack of bricks in your arms, six at first and up to twenty with skills. Walk to the wall, aim at the outlined spot and tap Place. A thin bed of mortar appears under every finished row.

Wall by wall, row by row

Buildings are split into sections: base course, each wall's lower and upper band, corner stones, windows, gables and roof. You may build them in any order; a brick only needs the bricks under it. Finished sections glow gold and give skill points.

Up to the roof

A scissor lift carries you along the wall to the high rows; you can even walk on the roof frame to lay the ridge. Tall buildings, like the clock tower at about 11 m, take the lift all the way up.

Tools that grow with you

  • Strong Arms: carry more bricks per trip.
  • Skilled Hands: lay each brick faster.
  • Brick Magnet: pull matching bricks straight to your arms.
  • Continuous Building: hold the button to lay brick after brick.

How big are the buildings?

The first, the Bakery, has about 520 bricks; later ones like the fire station and the train station have around 1,400. When the last brick goes in, the camera pulls back and replays the whole building rising.

Questions

Is there a realistic bricklaying game on iPhone?

Brickhaven is a first-person building game where every wall is made of individual bricks you carry and lay by hand, with blueprints, mortar rows and a scissor lift for high walls.

Can I make mistakes?

Yes, and fix them: a brick of the wrong color can be laid, turns red, and comes back with one tap. A section only completes when every brick matches the plan.
