Brickhaven Skill Tree: Which Skills to Unlock First

Every skill in Brickhaven explained: Strong Arms, Skilled Hands, the Brick Magnet, the lift and the rest, with a sensible order to spend your points.

You earn skill points by laying bricks correctly and by finishing sections and buildings. Open Skills from the top of the screen to spend them. The board has three columns, and you can reset it and spend the points again.

Carrying

Strong Arms

Carry more bricks per trip: from 6 at the start up to 20 at the last level.

Brick Magnet

A button that pulls bricks of the kind you are looking at straight into your arms: 1 brick at first, 10 at the last level, with a shorter wait each level.

Drop All

Hold Drop to put your whole load down at once.

Laying

Skilled Hands

Each brick goes into the wall faster.

Nonstop Laying

Hold the button and keep laying brick after brick.

Long Arms

Reach farther: place bricks up to 3 meters away instead of 2.1.

Moving

Sprint

Walk faster between the pile and the wall.

Lift

The lift is free and always reaches the roof; this skill makes it rise and travel faster.

High Jump

Jump higher, up to almost a meter.

A good order

  1. Strong Arms first. Every trip to the pile carries more, on every building.
  2. A few levels of Skilled Hands, so a full load goes into the wall quickly.
  3. The Brick Magnet as soon as it opens. Searching the pile is the slowest part on a phone.
  4. Sprint and Lift once the buildings get tall, from the Bookshop on.
  5. Nonstop Laying, Long Arms, Drop All and High Jump are comforts: take them when points are left over.

The story gives enough points for the whole board, so no choice is final.
