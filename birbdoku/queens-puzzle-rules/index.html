How to Play Queens: Rules of the Queens Logic Puzzle

The rules of the Queens puzzle (also known as Star Battle) explained simply: one bird per row, column and color, and birds never touch.

The rules

  1. Place exactly one bird in every row.
  2. Place exactly one bird in every column.
  3. Place exactly one bird in every colored region.
  4. Birds may not touch each other, not even diagonally.

On an N×N board there are N rows, N columns and N colored regions, so a solved board always has exactly N birds. A good puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved with logic alone, without guessing.

Queens is a version of the classic Star Battle puzzle with one star per line. It became popular as a quick daily game; some apps draw queens or crowns, Birbdoku uses two cockatiels, Pika and Chiko.

How to start

  1. Look for the smallest color or a color squeezed into one row or column.
  2. Mark cells that can't hold a bird with an X (tap once in Birbdoku; drag to mark many). X marks are as important as birds.
  3. Every time you place a bird (double-tap), mark its row, column, color and the eight cells around it.
  4. When you get stuck, look for a color whose open cells all sit in one line.
