How to play Queens

  1. Place exactly one bird in every row and every column.
  2. Place exactly one bird in every colored region.
  3. Birds may not touch each other, not even diagonally.
  4. Use X marks for cells that can't hold a bird. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, so you never have to guess.

Questions

When does a new puzzle come out?

Every day at 07:00 UTC, at the same moment for everyone in the world.

Is it the same puzzle as in the app?

Yes. The web puzzle is Birbdoku's daily puzzle (8×8). In the app the daily opens after level 20, next to the level path, hide-and-seek levels, Bird Duo, a streak garden and a weekly season.

Is it free?

Yes, today's puzzle is free here and in the app. Your progress is saved in this browser until the next puzzle.

Can I play more than one puzzle a day?

Yes, in the Birbdoku app for iPhone and iPad: the level path goes from 4×4 to 10×10 boards.
