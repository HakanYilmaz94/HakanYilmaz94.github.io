Is Birbdoku free?

Yes. Birbdoku is free to download and every level can be played without paying. It shows ads, and optional in-app purchases remove them or add helpers.

How do I play?

Tap a cell once to mark it with an X, tap it twice to place a bird. Every row, every column and every color needs exactly one bird, and birds may not touch, not even diagonally. Drag across the board to mark many cells.

What happens when I place a wrong bird?

You lose one of your three feathers. When all three are gone, start the level again or use a revive to carry on.

How does the weekly season work?

From level 11, every level you clear adds the feathers you kept (up to 3). You race up to 49 other players in a group of 50. A new season starts every Monday.

Someone has an offensive name in my group.

Touch and hold their row in the ranking to report the name or block the player.

Does it need the internet?

No for the puzzles. The season ranking and Game Center update when you are online.

Will my progress move to my new phone?

Yes. With "Save progress (iCloud)" on, your progress follows your Apple Account.

Is Birbdoku like LinkedIn Queens?

Yes, it uses the same rules (also known as Star Battle): one per row, column and colored region, never touching. Birbdoku draws birds instead of crowns and adds a level path, a daily puzzle and hints that explain the logic. It is not affiliated with LinkedIn.

Is there a cockatiel game?

Birbdoku stars two cockatiels: Pika, a lutino with a pink bow, and Chiko, a grey cockatiel. They play on the board and live in a garden that grows as you play.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later.

Which languages?

English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Dutch, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Traditional Chinese.
