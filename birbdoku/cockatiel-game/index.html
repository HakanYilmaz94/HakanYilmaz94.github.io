Cockatiel Game for iPhone: Birbdoku, a Cozy Puzzle With Two Cockatiels

Looking for a cockatiel game? Birbdoku is a cozy logic puzzle for iPhone and iPad starring two cockatiels, Pika and Chiko, with a garden that grows every day you play.

Birbdoku is a puzzle game made by a cockatiel fan for cockatiel fans. Its two heroes are cockatiels: Pika, a yellow lutino with a pink bow, and Chiko, a grey cockatiel with an orange cheek patch. They sit on the board, make a different face in every cell, cheer when you are right and wander around their garden between levels.

What you do in the game

Each level is a colored grid. You place one bird in every row, every column and every color, and birds may never touch, not even diagonally. Tap once to rule a cell out with an X, tap twice to place a bird. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, so it is pure logic, never guessing.

Every day you clear a level, Pika and Chiko's millet grows a little in their garden. Keep your streak for a week and you get a present; water bottles save a missed day.

The cockatiels

  • Pika: a lutino cockatiel (pale yellow and white) with long lashes and a pink bow.
  • Chiko: a grey cockatiel, the colour of wild cockatiels, with a pink beak and a grey crest.
  • Both have a set of faces (happy, curious, sleepy, proud…) and poses you can pick for your profile.
  • In the garden they walk, peck at seeds, nap, fly to the water bottle and react when you tap them.

About real cockatiels

Cockatiels are small parrots from Australia and the smallest members of the cockatoo family. They are known for the crest on their head, round orange cheek patches and their whistling, and they are one of the most popular pet birds in the world. Wild cockatiels are grey; lutino, pied and pearl birds are colour varieties bred by people.
