Daily Puzzle Games Like Wordle: 6 to Play Every Morning

Loved Wordle? Six daily puzzles with one new board a day, from word games to logic grids like Queens, and where to play them on iPhone.

Wordle's magic was simple: one puzzle a day, the same for everyone, done in a few minutes, then a small result to share. Plenty of games now follow that rhythm. Here are six, word and logic.

Word puzzles

  • Wordle (New York Times Games): guess a five-letter word in six tries.
  • Connections (New York Times Games): sort sixteen words into four hidden groups.

Logic puzzles

  • Queens (LinkedIn): one crown in every row, column and colored region; crowns never touch.
  • Zip (LinkedIn): draw one path through the numbered stops in order, filling every cell.
  • A daily sudoku: most sudoku apps and newspapers publish one board a day.

Birbdoku: a daily Queens puzzle with two cockatiels

Birbdoku puts the Queens rules on iPhone and iPad with birds instead of crowns: one bird in every row, column and color, and birds never touch. A new daily board comes out at 07:00 UTC, the same for everyone, and you can play today's board in your browser too.

Beyond the daily there is a long level path from 4×4 to 10×10, hide-and-seek levels, hints that explain the logic, a streak garden and a weekly season in groups of 50 players. It is free to play with ads.

Questions

Is there an app with a daily Queens puzzle?

Birbdoku has a daily Queens-style puzzle (one bird in every row, column and color) on iPhone and iPad, and the same board can be played on hyapps.app.

When do daily puzzles reset?

In Birbdoku, every day at 07:00 UTC, at the same moment for everyone in the world.
